# Detailed Solutions — Conceptual Questions (Module 04)
- **Q1 Solution**: `while` checks the condition before entering the body (executes `0` or more times); `do-while` checks the condition after executing the body (executes at least `1` time).
- **Q2 Solution**: Only integral types (`int`, `char`, `short`, `long`, `long long`, `bool`) and enumeration types (`enum`, `enum class`).
